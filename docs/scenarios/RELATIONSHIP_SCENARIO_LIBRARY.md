# Relationship Scenario Library

**Authority:** ACCEPTED PRODUCT TRUTH (templates)  
**Focus:** Harmony through coordination and private care work—not surveillance.

---

## Critical rule

**Shared relationship plan ≠ private work done to care for that relationship.**

| Shared (after agreement) | Private (default) |
|--------------------------|-------------------|
| Trip dates both accepted | Flowers / gift purchase |
| Dinner reservation they both know about | Personal spending limit |
| Hotel booking responsibility if they agreed to share it | Surprise necklace reminder |
| Anniversary dinner time | “Don’t forget the card” |

Surprise contexts exclude the guest from visibility.

---

## R1 — Romantic trip planning

Discuss trip → soft possibility → date candidates → destination unresolved → airfare/hotel commitments (“I’ll book”) → document deadlines → pet/childcare → packing lists (private or shared by choice).

Distinguish:

- “We should go to Paris someday” → possibility only  
- “Let’s go in October” → stronger proposal  
- “October 12 works” → time acceptance signal  
- “Book the hotel tonight” → commitment candidate  
- “I booked the hotel” → completion  

---

## R2 — Valentine’s preparation

May combine:

- **Shared:** dinner or travel they both planned  
- **Private:** flowers, gift, surprise timeline  

Opal must not put “buy flowers” on a shared event the partner can see.

---

## R3 — Birthday gift / preference memory

Partner once mentioned liking a necklace/restaurant/artist.

Later private surface:

> “You mentioned Maya liked the silver necklace. Want a private reminder before her birthday?”

Must not:

- claim they still want it with certainty  
- auto-purchase  
- expose to recipient  
- use for advertising  
- retain forever without purpose  

---

## R4 — Reservation responsibility

“I’ll book the hotel.” → commitment with:

- owner  
- privacy (private vs shared progress)  
- deadline  
- completion / transfer / withdrawal  

---

## R5 — Anniversary

Date awareness · private advance prep · optional shared celebration plan · no guilt scoring.

---

## R6 — Family coordination

Holiday travel · multi-household availability · school events · shared meals · circle isolation (family context ≠ work friendship).

Minors: **out of initial Social Flow architecture** (legal/safety track later).

---

## R7 — Restricted surprise (party / gift)

Organizer + helpers may see checklist.  
Guest must not see surprise plan.  
Leakage is a product defect.

---

## R8 — Follow-through after the moment

- Send pictures  
- Reimburse someone  
- “Do this again next month” soft possibility  
- Thank organizer  

Still consent-scoped; not nagging as default personality.

---

## Reusable primitives checklist

Every relationship scenario above must be implementable with:

PotentialPlan · AvailabilityGrant · TimeOption · PlanPoll · SharedPlan · ParticipationState · Commitment · PlanRevision · PersonalHold · Restricted surprise visibility · CalendarProjection  

No scenario-specific microservices required.
