Keeping these layers separate is what prevents Opal from collapsing into a generic AI assistant.

## Layer 1 — Reality Engine ✅

**Proven** (Pass 1 + Consumer Reality Gate GATE_GREEN).

Question: *What is actually happening?*

Includes: conversations · shared plans · commitments · attention states · surface consistency · realtime updates.

Foundation. Without this, intelligence is hallucination with a UI.

## Layer 2 — Understanding Framework ✅

**Defined, not implemented** (constitution + models).

Question: *What does this interaction mean?*

Guardrails — `OPAL_UNDERSTANDING_LAW.md` six questions:

1. Source  
2. Owner  
3. Visibility  
4. Confidence  
5. Correction  
6. Expiration  

Prevents: observation → permanent truth.

Example — “I could eat Italian food every day.”

**Forbidden:** `FACT: User loves Italian food.`  
**Required:** Observation / Source / Confidence / Scope / Status=Candidate / Correction / Expiration.

Also: `RELATIONSHIP_CONTEXT_MODEL.md` (Person + Context Graph, not flat profile).

## Layer 3 — Relationship Intelligence 🔒

**Locked** until founder unlock.

Highest uniqueness. Highest risk.

Unlock does **not** mean “turn on memory.”

Unlock means:

> Opal may begin creating **permissioned understanding objects.**

Refined unlock phrase:

> "Opal is ready to begin understanding people with explicit permission, transparent context, and user-controlled boundaries."

Legacy phrase still accepted if founder is explicit: *“Opal is ready to begin understanding people.”*

## Operating balance

**Aggressive capability, conservative authority.**

Opal may do impressive things. The user must always know:

- what Opal can access  
- why it is doing something  
- who it involves  
- how to change it  

Trust is the differentiator vs general assistants.

## Trust surface

Layer 2–3 product control lives on the **YOU page** (not a generic Settings menu): identity, world, permissions, relationships, and how Opal may work with the user. See `OPAL_PERMISSION_ARCHITECTURE.md`.
