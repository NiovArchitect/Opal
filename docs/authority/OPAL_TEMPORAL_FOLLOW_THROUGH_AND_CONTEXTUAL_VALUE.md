## Core product law

Home is a living projection of the user's world — not an engagement feed.

Optimize: `FOLLOW_THROUGH + USEFULNESS + ALIGNMENT`  
Minimize: `INTERRUPTION + NOISE + REDUNDANCY`  
Never rank primarily for session time, scroll depth, ad impressions, or CTR.
