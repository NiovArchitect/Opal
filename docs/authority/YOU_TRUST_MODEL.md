## Why YOU exists

Opal must not leave users asking:

> Is this an assistant? A social app? A profile? A settings app?

YOU resolves that. It is the place the user manages:

- themselves  
- their world  
- their relationships  
- their permissions  
- their relationship with Opal  

YOU is the **human-facing control plane**, not an app configuration panel. Configuring YOU means configuring how Opal participates in life.

## Trust questions (answered on YOU)

| Question | Where answered |
|----------|----------------|
| What does Opal know? | YOU |
| Why does Opal know it? | YOU |
| Who can see it? | YOU |
| Can I change it? | YOU |
| Can I remove it? | YOU |
| What can Opal do? | YOU |

When these are answerable, visible, and revocable, relationship intelligence is a feature. When they are not, it is a risk.

## Permission ≠ knowledge (restated)

Allowing Opal to process a category does **not** mean Opal “knows everything.”

It means Opal may form **Understanding Candidates** under Source · Owner · Visibility · Confidence · Correction · Expiration — then ask the user before Durable understanding.

> Normal AI: “I remember.”  
> Opal: “I noticed something. Is this important enough for me to understand?”

## Open model (do not freeze prematurely)

### Privacy may grow into Your Control

“Your Relationships → Privacy” is philosophically right today. Long-term privacy covers more than relationships (location, contacts, calendar, email, AI understanding, devices, shared experiences).

Keep the model open toward:

```text
Your Control
├── Privacy & Permissions
├── What Opal Understands
└── Who Can See What
```

Not required now. Do not block current Privacy card.

### How Opal Helps is the differentiator section

Today: Calls & Assist · Notifications · Appearance · Spending & Fit.

Eventually (after unlock + wiring), users should see what they can ask Opal to do — e.g. Conversations · Planning · Relationships · Places · Reminders · Health · Purchases · Automation — always with Access + Action layers (Suggest → Prepare → Execute).

### Spending and Fit — split cards, separate scopes

Hub now shows **Spending** and **Fit** as separate How Opal Helps cards (`USER_VS_CONTROL_LAW`).

| Domain | Meaning | Example scope rule |
|--------|---------|-------------------|
| **Spending** | Resource decisions (budget comfort, purchase prefs) | Partner may know “nicer date nights”; must not auto-know personal spend limit |
| **Fit** | Self-care (activity, wellness, habits) | Workout partner may know “morning runs”; not private wellness goals |

Future intelligence must not collapse these scopes.

### Grow Your Circle

Preferred over “Find People” — social growth, not a directory.

## Compounding sequence (do not rush the end)

```text
✅ Reality consistency
```
