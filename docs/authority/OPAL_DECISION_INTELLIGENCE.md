### Group ranking order

1. Hard-feasible intersection  
2. Protect weakest fit / fairness floor  
3. Maximize overall fit  
4. Relationship fit + useful novelty  
5. Minimize friction (travel / timing / coordination)  
6. Provider/reality truth beats aesthetic fit  

If no candidate clears hard constraints: surface conflict or ask the ONE resolving question.
