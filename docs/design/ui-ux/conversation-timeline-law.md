# Conversation is Opal’s primary social timeline

**Status:** product law (presentation)  
**PR #65:** continuity correction  
**Authority:** does not create a second domain/history engine  

---

## Law

> **The relationship thread is not merely where messages live. It is the human-readable history of alignment and action.**

Opal actions that arise from a conversation remain **anchored to that conversation** unless privacy or genuine complexity requires a temporary focused surface.

### Prefer

conversation → Opal materializes inline → user acts → Opal transforms → humans keep talking  

### Reject

chat → AI utility screen → task → return to chat  

---

## Exceptions

| Surface | Allowed? | Rule |
|---------|----------|------|
| Private time entry (owner-only) | Yes, temporary | Turns **inward** from chat, contracts back, never dumps raw private times into shared history |
| Complex future: trip, payment, deep provider | Maybe | Still linked to originating relationship/thread |

---

## Ephemeral vs historical

| Ephemeral (controls) | Historical (meaning) |
|----------------------|----------------------|
| Find a time chip | Shared-safe result: “Thursday could work” |
| Expanded possibility options | Chosen shared detail after agreement |
| Private field / sheet | **Set** with confirmed detail |
| Ambient motion | Quiet static Opal markers |

Do not preserve every UI artifact. Preserve **meaningful state changes**.

---

## Visual ages

| Age | Treatment |
|-----|-----------|
| **live** | Ambient, actionable, one at a time |
| **recent** | Clear, settling, less motion |
| **historical** | Quiet, static, reduced saturation — still Opal |

Motion plays on **state transition**, not on scroll-into-view replay.

---

## One active surface

Historical quiet markers may exist above.  
Only **one** current Opal intervention gets motion + action priority.

---

## Privacy

Shared thread receives only shared-safe projections.  
Private raw availability stays private.
