## Product identity

Opal is **not** messenger, calendar, assistant, chatbot, or social feed.  
It is an **AI-native social communication layer**: conversation → understanding → alignment → coordination → real-world experience → memory → faster future alignment.

**Moat:** permissioned multi-person intelligence + real-world execution.

## North star

**Speed to authentic alignment**

- Faster true understanding between people (and solo self-fit).  
- Never rushed or fake agreement.  
- Protect truth, consent, privacy, dignity, safety, relationship trust.

## Dopamine (allowed)

Anticipation, recognition, reciprocity, momentum, relief, surprise (fitting options), completion, continuity.  
**Not:** points, streaks, rankings, guilt, endless feed.

- First seconds must feel: *what is this / beautiful / understands people / show friends*.  
- OPAL wordmark commands; giant decorative orb is suspect.  
- Brand arrival before “Life starts in conversation.”  
- No redundant top-left logo after arrival.  
- Join must be powerful.  
- Becoming a plan = category demo.  
- Never blur human vs Opal proposal vs Opal action vs provider confirmation.
