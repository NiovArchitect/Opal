**What It Is:**

**\- Personalization Engine:**  
  \- The AI uses the staked data to create a dynamic profile of each user. This profile is continuously updated based on interactions, feedback, and new data points, ensuring that recommendations stay relevant.  
  \- The system identifies overlapping interests and preferences among your contacts and suggests shared activities that enhance your connection. For example, if you and a friend both stake your DID with an interest in music, the calendar might suggest a concert that fits both your tastes.

**Example:**  
Imagine you and a close friend both enjoy outdoor activities. You’ve both staked DIDs that highlight this interest.
