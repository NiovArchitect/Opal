# Experience collaboration and personal nuance

**Status:** Product truth for Social Flow 17 continuation  
**Authority:** Elixir decides eligibility, visibility, and lifecycle. Python may propose only.

## Product model

Opal is an ambient social-intelligence layer that helps people collaborate with one another’s experiences.

Four connected states:

1. **Relationship state** — who is connected, what may be shared.
2. **Journey state** — what people are discussing, preparing, experiencing, or remembering.
3. **Nuance state** — preferences, constraints, traditions, meaningful details.
4. **Participation state** — interested, flexible, confirmed, waiting, sitting out.

Opal is not a calendar, not a chatbot inside every bubble, and not traditional social media.

## Journey signals (shared progress)

Signals describe **conversation meaning**, never a person’s identity.

Examples:

| Stage | Example label |
|-------|----------------|
| Quiet | (no signal) |
| Forming | Becoming a plan |
| Partial | Still open |
| Deferred | Will know later |
| Aligned | Ready |
| Complete | Handled |

Absence of a signal is valid and preferred when nothing meaningful needs attention.

Placement:

- Conversation list: optional journey chip (not under a name as identity).
- Thread: one dominant journey bar + occasional inline Opal moment.
- Plans tab: “In motion” list of current shared progress.

## Content classes

| Class | Who sees it | Visual |
|-------|-------------|--------|
| Human message | Participants | Ordinary chat bubbles |
| Shared Opal moment | Authorized participants | Compact luminous capsule |
| Private Opal guidance | One user only | Private surface only |
| System confirmation | Actor | Short system note |

Opal must never place AI text inside ordinary human bubbles as if it were a person.

## Personal nuance

Nuance classes (bounded): preference, constraint, interest, dislike, comfort, availability pattern, gift clue, accessibility, tradition, place affinity, group compatibility.

Each nuance record must carry: owner, source context, visibility, purpose, relationship scope, confidence, freshness, correction/revocation state, sensitivity class.

Language:

- Prefer: mentioned, may prefer, previously enjoyed, worth checking.
- Avoid: always likes, definitely wants (unless explicitly confirmed).

Python proposes. Elixir admits, holds private, shares, expires, or discards.

## Surprise-sensitive privacy

Gift research, proposals, birthdays, secret trips:

- Private guidance never appears in the recipient’s thread.
- Never broadcast on sockets as shared progress.
- Never fold into shared summaries without explicit permission.

## Healthy reward loop

Rewards come from social reality, not gamification:

- a reply arrives
- an idea gains momentum
- someone is remembered thoughtfully
- a group finds a fit
- uncertainty becomes clarity
- a plan becomes real
- a surprise stays protected
- an experience becomes a memory

No points, streaks, rankings, reliability scores, or follower counts.

## Noise budget

Default: quiet. Signals must earn visibility through relevance, timing, shared usefulness, actionability, uncertainty reduction, social value, and privacy safety.
