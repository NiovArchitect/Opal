# Opal Experience Collaboration

**Authority:** FOUNDER-DIRECTED PRODUCT TRUTH (Social Flow 17)  
**Status:** Accepted product concept. Foundation documented. Full domain expansion continues in later slices.

---

## Locked statement

> **Opal helps people collaborate with one another’s experiences.**

This is product truth, not a slogan to repeat on every screen.

People do not only collaborate on tasks or calendar slots. They collaborate with:

- interests and preferences
- schedules and energy
- memories and traditions
- hopes and comfort levels
- discoveries and celebrations
- changing circumstances

An experience may begin as a thought, a message, a joke, a wish, a place, a meal, a gift idea, an invitation, a tradition, or a moment someone wants to repeat.

Opal helps people move naturally from conversation into shared experience.

This is **not** calendar collaboration alone. It is **experience collaboration**.

---

## Four connected state forms

| State | Meaning | Authority |
|-------|---------|-----------|
| **Relationship** | Who is connected, context, permissions | Elixir |
| **Journey** | What people are considering, coordinating, experiencing, remembering | Elixir |
| **Nuance** | Preferences, constraints, interests, traditions, meaningful details | Elixir stores; Python may propose |
| **Participation** | Interested, flexible, likely, confirmed, observing, sitting out | Elixir |

BEAM maintains these states in realtime. Elixir enforces visibility and consent. Python proposes meaning from conversation. Python never decides shared truth alone.

---

## Healthy social reward (no gamification)

Reward comes from:

- anticipation of a real plan forming
- recognition that something meaningful was noticed
- relief when a loop closes
- shared momentum
- completed experiences

Forbidden:

- points, streaks, rankings, relationship scores
- engagement bait, guilt copy, fake urgency
