# Social Communication AI — Product Truth (SF18)

Opal is an AI-native **social communication** system.

## Hierarchy

1. People  
2. Conversation  
3. Shared meaning  
4. Experience  
5. Opal assistance  

## What it is

- Human conversation stays primary  
- Opal observes only eligible context  
- Useful social meaning becomes calm, bounded moments  
- Silence is valid  

## What it is not

- Not a chatbot between people  
- Not a public feed  
- Not a follower graph  
- Not an address-book harvester  
- Not a calendar  
- Not a task manager  
- Not constant analysis or surveillance  

## First magic after relationship

After acceptance and a meaningful plan-like exchange, a single shared Opal moment (e.g. Becoming a plan) may appear. Ordinary talk stays quiet.
