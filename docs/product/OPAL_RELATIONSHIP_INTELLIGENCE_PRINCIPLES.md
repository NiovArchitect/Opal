# Opal Relationship Intelligence Principles

**Authority:** ACCEPTED PRODUCT TRUTH  
**Scope:** How Opal may help relationships without becoming surveillance, scoring, or a dating-health product.

---

## Core distinction

Opal embeds **relationship intelligence inside private communication**.

It helps people:

- understand and coordinate;
- remember commitments;
- prepare thoughtfully;
- repair friction;

It does **not**:

- score people or relationships;
- claim certainty about another’s private thoughts;
- run always-on emotional surveillance;
- monetize intimate moments via dark patterns or ad targeting on private content.

---

## Relationship-first starting point

Begin with people the user already communicates with most—and **let the user confirm labels**.

Acceptable:

> “You talk with Jordan often. Would you like Opal to help coordinate plans and commitments in this conversation?”

Not acceptable:

> “Jordan is your most important relationship.”

Expansion order (gradual):

1. Closest confirmed relationships  
2. Trusted small groups  
3. Family circles  
4. Broader friends  
5. Professional/community  
6. Public discovery only much later, opt-in  

---

## Shared vs private intelligence

| Kind | Who sees | Consent |
|------|----------|---------|
| Private reflection / gift prep / “buy flowers” | Only the user | That user |
| Shared plan after agreement | Participants | Dual/shared rules as defined per feature |
| Proposal (pre-commit) | Initiator, optionally with soft share | Explicit coordinate consent |
| Restricted surprise context | Organizer + helpers **excluding guest** | Explicit surprise boundary |

See `docs/architecture/SOCIAL_FLOW_PRIVACY_BOUNDARIES.md`.

---

## Uncertainty and facts

Allowed private recall of **facts**:

> “Jordan cancelled the last two Thursday plans.”

Forbidden:

> “Jordan has a 62 reliability score.”

Opal may support judgment; Opal is not the judge.

---

## High-trust actions

Require explicit approval (or a narrowly scoped, user-authored standing rule that is still visible and revocable):

- Accept/decline invitations on the user’s behalf  
- Notify a group of a time change  
- Share free/busy beyond stated grant  
- Expose private preparation to others  
- Create or cancel a shared plan
