## Three paths to a usable Shared Reality

| Path | Human state | Opal posture |
|------|-------------|--------------|
| **Known intent** | They know what they want | Remove logistics; collapse remaining gaps |
| **Known vibe / partial** | Area, time, vibe; details open | Quiet composition; one remaining decision when needed |
| **Delegated curation** | “We want to be together” + **explicit** handoff | Compose one coherent experience |

**Delegation is authority.** Opal must **never** infer indecision and take creative control. Humans must explicitly hand Opal the creative/logistical choice.

Conceptual affordances (copy TBD): “Curate it” · “Plan our evening” · natural language “Opal, plan it.”

## 1. Delegated curation

When humans know they want to be together (and maybe area / time / vibe) but not exactly what to do, Opal may take **delegated creative responsibility** after explicit invitation.

They are saying: **“We trust you. Figure this out for us.”**

This is **not** ordinary recommendation. It is composition under trust, inside the **existing Alignment system**.

### Curation is not recommendation sprawl

**Must not mean:** 17 restaurants, 12 activities, search results, feed, itinerary builder.

**Means:** Opal privately composes **one coherent experience**, then compresses it.

Example private composition:

- 7:00 · dinner · Juniper & Ivy  
- 8:45 · short waterfront walk  
- 9:30 · dessert nearby  

User-facing compression:

> **I’ve got your evening.**  
> Dinner · walk · dessert  
> Thursday · 7 PM · Little Italy  

Optional: inspect details · **Looks good** · **Change the vibe** (only if irreducible preference remains).

On accept → same **SharedPlan / Shared Reality** architecture. Provenance may record `delegated_to_opal`. **No separate “curated plan” product.**

### Do not create

`CurationAuthority` · `ItineraryEngine2` · `ExperiencePlannerAuthority` · SocialAuthorshipAuthority · DatingLeaderEngine · PaymentRoleEngine · TimezoneIntelligence2.

### Compose existing

intent · relationship context · SocialContext · CompoundAlignment · CollectiveFit · world facts · location/travel · Readiness · ExecutionContext · PlanLifecycle · Memory · Freshness · TrustFact · PlanVersion.

**AlignmentAuthority remains sole Set authority.**

## 2. Vibe / experience intent

Utterances like chill · cute · fun · romantic · lively · dress up · casual · quick · adventurous · quiet · spontaneous are **experience intent**, not crude UI categories.

Preserve meaning through place → activity → timing → execution.

**A logistically perfect plan that violates vibe is not high-quality alignment.**

Shared Reality closure must close **emotional/social expectation**, not logistics alone.

## 3. Social context changes curation (4D)

Same city, same 7 PM — different composition for:

courtship · close friends · established partner · family · coworkers · birthday group · casual acquaintances.

Influence without crude permanent badges unless useful:

venue fit · duration · participant flexibility · travel tolerance · activity sequencing · expectations · privacy.

**Person × Relationship × Time × World** is the product matrix, not decoration.

## 4. Extend the experience

Once a Shared Reality is **occurring**, Opal may support **explicit continuation** (copy TBD): Extend · Keep going · What next?

**User-requested.** Not automatic nightlife pushing. Not engagement farming.

### Uses present reality

current place · current time · still open · walking distance · relationship context · current Shared Reality · later commitments · transport · trusted preferences · reliable world heat.

Compress to **one strong continuation** (or one true tradeoff). Never nearby-search feed.

### May return nothing

Longer ≠ better. If nothing improves the evening: **stay quiet** or minimal (“You’re already in a good spot”). No activity farming.

### Authority bounds

Delegation/Extend does **not** grant: automatic booking · payment · sharing private facts · participant changes · Set mutation without authority path. AVP² / execution / permission boundaries remain.

### Participant change during curated experience

“Can Olivia join?” → recompute only affected dimensions (capacity, booking, social context, transport, privacy, hard constraints). Preserve still-true facts. If meaning of the experience changes materially → smallest human decision.

## 5. Human language (not status vocabulary)

### Internal (keep precise)

`forming` · `still_open` · `set` · `ready` · `execution_ready` · `completed` · …

### Human-facing

**When reality can speak for itself, do not show status.**

| Avoid as primary meaning | Prefer |
|--------------------------|--------|
| Set | Dinner with Maya · Thursday · 7 PM · Herb & Wood |
| Open / Still open | Thursday works · finding the right place — or quiet |
| This could work | Thursday works for both of you |
| Needs you | **Harbor Table or Campfire?** (the actual decision) |

Do **not** invent a new user-facing status dictionary. Backend terms stay; UI stops leading with them.

**Child-simple language:** understandable to a socially active young teenager; premium for adults. No workflow jargon, planning-product vocabulary, or developer-state words.

**PR #112** already moves in this direction via SharedRealityPresentation; preserve the law.

## 6. Opal chronological action (conversation history)

**Critical.** When Opal performs a socially relevant action because of a conversation, that action belongs **chronologically in the conversation** with real event timestamps.

Not a detached AI activity log. Not only a Plans dashboard.

Scroll months later:

> human intent → Opal recognition/action → human decision → Shared Reality

**Causal recall** for trust. Consequence tells the story — no verbose AI audit trail.

Human-friendly times only. No ISO. No debug seconds.

## 9. Social authorship (relationship-specific)

**Coordination authority, social authorship, and financial responsibility are relationship-specific** — not assumed from gender, and not collapsed into one field.

| Concept | Meaning |
|---------|---------|
| **Decision authority** | Who may decide |
| **Social authorship** | Who the choice should appear to come from socially |
| **Financial responsibility** | Who intends to pay / authorize money |

### Two postures

1. **Opal-assisted human leadership** — Opal does ~95% privately; social gesture remains human (e.g. partner sees “Sadeil suggested …”).  
2. **Explicitly delegated Opal curation** — Opal may step forward: “I put something together for you.”

### Provenance law

> **Opal may work invisibly, but it must not falsify who made a meaningful social decision.**

- If Opal chose under `delegated_to_opal` → do not pretend a human selected.  
- If human selected after Opal options → do not announce Opal as chooser.  
- Chooser ≠ payer; current explicit payment intent overrides history.  
- Relationship-specific leadership does **not** globalize.

Personalization is **not** putting a name in a sentence. It is understanding **role in the relationship**.

Do not create SocialAuthorshipAuthority — use Memory, Relationship/Social/Alignment context, thin provenance metadata.

## 11. Timezone & location laws (boringly correct)

### Time

- Preserve precise **instant (UTC)** + **IANA event timezone** (`America/Los_Angeles`).  
- **Event-local time** is the social truth of the plan.  
- Participant device TZ / temporary travel TZ affect display/feasibility/reminders — **must not mutate agreed event time**.  
- No fixed PST/UTC-8 as canonical; handle DST via IANA.  
- Social UI: `7:30 PM` — never `19:30:00-07:00`, never ISO, never seconds.

### Location meanings (do not collapse)

| Kind | Notes |
|------|--------|
| **Current location** | Ephemeral, private |
| **Expected origin** | Private leave-from for this plan |
| **Meeting area / place** | Shared-safe when part of Shared Reality |
| **Destination travel** | Feasibility / leave-by |

Current location change updates feasibility, not agreed time. Private origin never leaks.

## Final law

Opal is not only aligning logistics. It operates **inside human relationships**.

It must understand not only **what should happen**, but:

**who should decide · who should socially lead · who should see which assistance · who authorizes money · who actually made the choice · how that truth is presented.**

Opal can do 95% of the work while still letting a human be the person who made the thoughtful plan.

When humans **explicitly** delegate, Opal may step forward and show what it can do.

**Authority, authorship, payment, and provenance are different.**

**Person × Relationship × Time × World.**  
Timezone + location must be boringly correct.  
Human UI must remain beautifully simple.

**Know deeply. Present naturally. Never falsify who did what.**

> **Personalization isn't putting someone's name into a sentence.  
> Personalization is understanding their role in the relationship.**

> Invisible intelligence when humans want authorship.  
> Visible intelligence when humans delegate.

If they know the details — remove logistics.  
If they know the vibe — collapse gaps.  
If they explicitly delegate — curate.  
If the night is going well and they ask — extend (or stay quiet).

Result should feel: **“Opal has us.”**  
Not: **“Opal gave us an itinerary builder.”**
