# User Problems

**Status:** Phase 0  
**Primary user (MVP):** Consenting adults in important personal relationships (partners, family, close friends) who already message daily and still lose meaning, plans, and repair opportunities.

---

## Core problem

People communicate constantly on phones, yet:

1. **Meaning is lost** — tone, intent, and subtext do not survive text well.  
2. **Commitments evaporate** — “I’ll call tonight” becomes ambient guilt, not support.  
3. **Misunderstandings compound** — avoidant or indirect wording is misread; repair is delayed.  
4. **Coordination is friction** — plans bounce across apps and half-threads.  
5. **Language barriers are cold** — literal translation without tone.  
6. **Tools that “help” feel like surveillance or dashboards** — users do not want to be scored.

Opal’s job is to reduce avoidable relational friction **inside** the place people already talk—without becoming creepy, certain, or controlling.

## Problem → Opal response map

| User pain | Desired outcome | Opal response (signal) | Anti-pattern |
|-----------|-----------------|------------------------|--------------|
| “That came out wrong.” | Clearer expression | Help me say this better | Auto-send rewrite |
| “I don’t know how they took it.” | Possible readings | May have been read differently… | “They are angry at you” certainty |
| “What did we decide?” | Shared clarity | Recap / decision candidate | Hidden memory they can’t see |
| “I forgot I promised.” | Follow-through | You said you would… (confirmed) | Silent obligation creation |
| “We’re stuck scheduling.” | Easy coordination | You both seem free after… | Another calendar product |
| “I don’t speak their language.” | Natural understanding | Translate + show original | Hide source language |
| “I’m too angry to reply.” | Harm reduction | Slow-down / safer drafts | Shame or therapy theater |
| “AI is watching us.” | Trust | Explicit consent, revoke, private vs shared | Always-on analysis |

## Primary personas (MVP-oriented)

### A. The Daily Partner

Messages all day with one person. Wants less conflict from tone and fewer dropped plans. Values privacy fiercely.

### B. The Cross-Language Friend / Family

Loves someone across a language barrier. Wants warmth preserved in translation, not robot English/Spanish.

### C. The Overcommitted Coordinator

Juggles family and friends. Needs commitments and soft plans without living in calendar grids.

### Non-goals for primary persona

- Public creator growth  
- Workplace team productivity as core  
- Dating marketplace matching  

---

## First value moment (target)

Within the first real conversation after connect:

1. Message delivers instantly (trust: “this is a real messenger”).  
2. Optional: transcript or translation helps without friction.  
3. One **useful, dismissible** relationship assist appears (e.g., commitment candidate or rephrase help) that the user can accept or ignore.

If Opal only feels like “another chat app,” intelligence is failing.  
If Opal only feels like “an AI coach,” messaging is failing.

---

## Jobs to be done (selected)

1. When I message someone I care about, help me be understood without sounding like a robot.  
2. When something is left hanging, surface it gently at the right time.  
3. When I commit, help me follow through without nagging.  
4. When I’m flooded emotionally, help me not send something I will regret.  
5. When we plan, keep it in the relationship thread—not a separate productivity silo.  
6. When languages differ, let us talk as ourselves.  

---

## Success signals (product, not vanity)

- Users keep Opal as a **primary** 1:1 channel for at least one important relationship.  
- High rate of **dismiss without resentment** for AI suggestions (suggestions are optional).  
- Measurable reduction in “forgot to reply / forgot to call” self-reports (research).  
- Consent revoke remains easy; abuse of AI features is rare.  

Non-success: high engagement with “relationship scores,” fear-based notifications, or AI that users feel they must argue with.
