## Specialist artifacts (material findings)

### Product Algorithm Architect

- Core loop accepted: conversation → understanding → curation → real-world experience → digital continuity → better understanding.  
- Circles must be **dynamic contexts**, not primary CRUD.  
- Collective fit must be multi-constraint satisfaction, not mean averaging.  
- Phase 1 fixture dinner scenario is the correct narrow proof.

### Experience Curation Architect

- Output cardinality: 0–3 options, prefer 1.  
- “42 restaurants” pattern rejected.  
- Continuity after the experience (shared memory, soft follow-up) is part of the product, not a separate app mode.

### Location Privacy Architect

- Scopes table accepted (approximate, session, experience-only, private ranking, never show peers).  
- Continuous public tracking rejected.  
- Phase 1 uses fixtures only; no GPS claim.

### Privacy and Data Governance Reviewer

- Private constraints must not leak in shared “See why.”  
- Mention ≠ permission (aligns with permissioned knowledge Phase 0).  
- Revocation priority highest in learning engine.  
- Surprise-sensitive paths remain private.

### Elixir and OTP Architect

- Admission, privacy, restraint final gates stay on BEAM.  
- Python proposals only.  
- Execution side effects only after explicit confirmation.

### Python Intelligence Architect

- Proposals must carry confidence, uncertainty language, and feature provenance for audit.  
- No autonomous chat personae.  
- Fixture-based Phase 1 is sufficient to prove ranking shape.

### Kafka and Foundation Architect

- Foundation/Kafka remain separated.  
- Not required for Phase 0 truth or Phase 1 narrow proof.  
- Do not block SF18 on foundation bridge.

### Provider Integration Architect

- Providers enter only after human confirmation.  
- Never as chat participants.  
- Commercial influence is a restraint penalty factor.

### Creator and Audience Architect

- Two-graph model preserved.  
- Audience follow does not grant relationship-level nuance.  
- Creator knowledge stays under permissioned knowledge rules.

### Youth Safety Reviewer

- Dynamic experience intelligence **off** for minor-involved threads until dedicated gates pass.  
- Phase 1 scenario is adult-only by design.

### Brand Guardian / Copy Reviewer

- No em dashes in product-facing copy.  
- No technical jargon (“collective-fit score”, “graph edge”) in UI.  
- Preferred copy: short, warm, optional, never bossy.

### UX Researcher / Age-14 Comprehension Reviewer

- Primary surface must be understandable at a glance: promising / not now / why / private.  
- Configuration homework fails age-14 comprehension and adult patience alike.
