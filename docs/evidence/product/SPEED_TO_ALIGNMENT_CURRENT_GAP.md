**Author:** Grok  
**Base:** main `c177ba6`  
**North star:** Speed to **authentic** alignment (not rushed decisions)

## Definition used

Alignment = shared enough truth among the right people to move together with less friction, while private constraints stay private.

## Alignment friction in current product

1. **No explicit AlignmentGap object** in product language or durable model.
2. **Minimum-question UX not productized** — intelligence may evaluate, but user-facing “one light ask” is not the center of the shell.
3. **Human vs Opal vs execution grammar incomplete** in walkthrough demos (Peak Brand Phase 0 defects; Grok-authored, not Claude-reviewed).
4. **Solo alignment underdeveloped** — empty member + Find People exists; no strong solo discovery loop.
5. **Location** — not live; no ETA/centrality ranking in production.
6. **Provider execution** — not live; cannot complete restaurant “Booked” truthfully outside synthetic claims.
7. **Production SMS / real invite delivery** — blocked for consumer scale.
8. **Learning** — Phase 3 in source; not proven hosted with production migrations.

## False speed (reject)

- Pushing Join without trust.
- Fake booking language without authority.
- Ranking that exposes private constraints.
- Engagement dopamine (streaks, scores) instead of social progress.

## Gap priority for vertical slice

Three-person local possibility + private constraint + undecided participant + location makes option poor → one private question → better shared-safe option → Still open → Ready. No forms. No private leak.
