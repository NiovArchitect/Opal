## Black opal preference (explore, not locked)

Living Void UI is dark/premium. A **black/dark opal** interior (cyan / violet / emerald flashes, rare warm spark) can feel rare and dimensional without neon AI rainbow.

Test white/fire opal for comparison before locking color.
