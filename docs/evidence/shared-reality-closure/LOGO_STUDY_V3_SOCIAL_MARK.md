## Craft laws (locked)

### One object

Eye must not read: parenthesis + star + parenthesis.  
Eye must read: **ONE OPAL SYMBOL**.

Whole composition **is** Opal — Moment is not “the logo” with arcs as decoration.

### Meaning

```
OPEN SOCIAL SIDES  +  OWNABLE MOMENT
= SOCIAL POSSIBILITY BECOMING SHARED REALITY
```

- **Open sides:** social sides / perspectives / possibilities (not only pair-wise)  
- **Moment:** resolution point — uncertainty collapses enough to move  
- **Material:** changing context (people × relationship × time × place)

Short: **YOUR PEOPLE · THE RIGHT MOMENT · SOMETHING REAL.**

### Geometry craft

- Openness retained — never close the ring  
- Arcs: social, human, intentional, slightly organic, premium  
- Avoid: parentheses · headphones · loading ring · letter C’s  
- Moment: vertical-first · waist compression · not AI sparkle / star emoji  
- Small-size optical (≤32px): slightly stronger Moment, closer arcs, robust stroke — same identity  

### Color

Primary: violet → electric blue → cyan  
Secondary: selective pink  
Rare: warm/fire  
Light **inside** form — not rainbow print, not outer halo  

### Wordmark W1

Young · social · premium · fast · legible  
Not gaming / cyberpunk / enterprise tech / futuristic gimmick  

- Proprietary **A**: raised crossbar + tiny center compression (Moment waist kinship) — **not** star insert  
- O/P/L system coherence; optical spacing  
- Symbol and wordmark independent  

### Presence

Logo scarcity. Opal material in product often carries identity.  
Interface must not become logo-heavy.

### Brand vs filament

Logo = brand identity.  
Filament = intelligence behavior. Same family, different jobs.

### Accessibility

High-contrast mono always. Static equivalent to motion. Meaning never depends on iridescence.
