## 1. Product definition (use this)

Opal is an **AI-powered social coordination app** that helps people **actually do the things they talk about doing**.

It understands conversations, people, schedules, preferences, location, and context, then quietly helps turn vague intentions into real plans with less back-and-forth.

### Core line

**Opal turns social possibility into shared reality.**

### Category contrast

- Instagram shows you what people did.  
- Messaging apps let you talk about what you might do.  
- **Opal helps your people actually make it happen.**

### Build law

**AI does more work. Humans experience less software.**

Compress coordination into the smallest meaningful decision, execute when authorized, then get out of the way.

### Do not use as primary human-facing dialect

`Set` · `Open` · `Still open` · `This could work` · `Needs you`  

These may exist **internally**. They are not the public social dialect.

**Human example:**

> Dinner with Jordan · Thursday · 7:00 PM · Herb & Wood  

not `SET`.  

> Jordan and I still need a place  

not `Still open`.
