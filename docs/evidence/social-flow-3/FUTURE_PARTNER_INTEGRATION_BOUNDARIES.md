# Future Partner Integration Boundaries

**Authority:** ARCHITECTURE (FUTURE — not implemented)  
**Social Flow 3:** **zero** partner APIs, ads, purchases, affiliate links, or sponsored recommendations.

---

## Allowed future partner classes (when product chooses)

Restaurants, venues, tickets, travel, transport, hotels, local experiences, florists, gifts, family activities, accessibility-friendly venues, community events — **only** to reduce friction on an **active** social objective the user is pursuing.

---

## Partner relevance gates (all required before any show)

1. Real active social objective  
2. Recommendation resolves an open detail  
3. User asked for options or enabled capability  
4. Consented private context only  
5. No sensitive relationship content to partner  
6. Partner never receives raw conversation text  
7. Sponsored clearly labeled  
8. Organic vs sponsored distinguishable  
9. Dismissible  
10. Dismiss does not degrade core function  
11. No artificial urgency  
12. No Social Score ranking  
13. No child-directed commercial targeting  
14. No intimate-data advertising  
15. Journey completable without the partner  

---

## Commercial integrity

- Paid placement cannot override relevance  
- Non-sponsored path always available  
- Minimum necessary data to partner  
- No raw conversation sale  
- No relationship-profile sale  
- No emotional-state targeting  
- No private gift-memory targeting  
- No pay-to-win social visibility  

**Feel:** “Opal helped us finish the plan.”  
**Not:** “Opal used our conversation to sell us something.”

---

## Incorrect product pattern

Detect romantic chat → insert sponsored restaurant without ask.

## Correct future pattern

Users agree dinner → location open → **explicit** “Show places near both of us” → small useful set under consent and labels.
