# Brand-arrival concepts

## Concept A — Refractive formation
Channels align into OPAL. Meaning: many signals → one understanding.  
Timing: 250ms motion · ~1.2s brand · ~2.5s line. Reduced-motion: static spectral mark + word.  
Risk: generic logo intro if slow.

## Concept B — Conversation to Opal (recommended)
Fragments (dinner? / after 7 / not loud) converge into mark + OPAL.  
Meaning: conversation becomes intelligence. Best category demo.

## Concept C — Experience ignition
Point of light expands into relational paths → OPAL.  
Meaning: one conversation begins an experience. Risk: abstract AI.

## Decision
**Prototype default: B**, with L2/L3 logo hierarchy (command wordmark, no giant orb).
