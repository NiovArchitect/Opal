# B3 — You / Section 06 Intent Lock

**Does not grant design freedom.** Preserves why control surfaces exist.

## Purpose

You is the user-governed **trust boundary** of Opal:

IDENTITY · CONTROL · PRIVACY · PERSONALIZATION GOVERNANCE · SAFETY · USER AGENCY

**Reward moment:** “Opal knows me, but I remain in control.”

Clarity and trust take precedence over decorative complexity — still unmistakably Brand V4 (sparse accents on Midnight).

## Visual hierarchy (every screen)

1. What can I govern here?  
2. Why does it matter for my privacy / fit / safety?  
3. What is the easiest safe control?

## Critical AI trust law (survives B3)

Private intelligence used by Opal ≠ what other people may see.

| May use privately | Must not auto-expose socially |
|-------------------|-------------------------------|
| Location for ETA / nearby / solo | Live location share (off by default) |
| Budget for ranking | “Maya only has $35” in group |
| Rejected venues | Public preference statement |
| Past moments for relevance | Auto-public Memory |

Opal may say “Works for the group” without revealing private constraints.

Additive Figma `902:*` states are **not** implemented in B3 — but Section 06 must remain coherent so those states stay trustworthy later.
