# SF14 — Brand & Logo

## Mark: Lumen Lens

Soft luminous orb with iridescent cyan/iris/pearl sheen and open connection arcs.

### Communicates
- Clarity and social signal
- Subtle luminescence (futuristic, calm)
- Conversation becoming something more
- Human warmth (pearl note)

### Rejects
- WhatsApp speech-bubble phone
- Telegram plane, Signal lock-bubble, Discord game mark
- Crypto/neon cyber glyphs
- Literal gemstone cut cliché
- Detail that dies at favicon size
