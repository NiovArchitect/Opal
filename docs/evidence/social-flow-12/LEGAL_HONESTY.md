# Social Flow 12 — Release Honesty

This build may establish an **internally validated mobile release candidate**.

It does **not** constitute:

- App Store approval  
- Google Play approval  
- legal certification  
- production telecom assurance  
- comprehensive security certification  
- unrestricted youth-production authorization  
- production SLA  
- perfect device compatibility  

Validation used synthetic fixtures and high-fidelity viewport/simulator-class profiles. Physical-device coverage was not complete on every hardware model.
