# Ambient Opportunity Intelligence

**ADD-ON** to Physical Reality + existing Alignment stack. Not a redesign.

## Six locked concepts

1. **Social Opening** — people + time + willingness + physical feasibility
2. **Opportunity Density** — relevant possibility around context (no heat-map UI)
3. **Actionability** — interesting → … → actionable → execution_ready
4. **Alignment Compression** — complexity → fewest human decisions
5. **Execution Readiness** — social Set ≠ provider ready ≠ authorized execute
6. **Opportunity Expiry** — sourced urgency only; never manufactured

## Group viability (not pure majority)

- Optional miss does not freeze plans
- Required participants can block viability (birthday, driver, host)
- No holdout shaming; graceful miss; re-enter future OK
- Late join/decline without full restart

## Heat (internal only)

- World × Network × Relationship → contextual trending
- Never three meters, never heat-map tab

## Surface rule

> Opal surfaces the world when it becomes unusually easy to act on with people you care about.

Silence is success. Existing Restraint + conversation grammar only.
