# Social Flow 10 — Legal, Telecom, and Identity Honesty

This is a **synthetic development foundation** for communication identity, contact matching, invitations, and relationship establishment.

It is **not**:

- legal identity verification  
- government identity verification  
- production Know Your Customer compliance  
- universal phone-number ownership assurance  
- carrier-level fraud prevention  
- telecom regulatory certification  
- comprehensive SIM-swap prevention  
- comprehensive account-takeover prevention  
- production SMS deliverability  
- production youth onboarding approval  
- production spam-prevention assurance  
- comprehensive contact-discovery privacy certification  

Production use requires qualified telecom providers, abuse monitoring, number-reassignment handling, jurisdiction-specific privacy review, operational support, and broader adversarial testing.
