# Social Flow 9 — Legal, Safety, and Assurance Honesty

This is a **synthetic development trust-and-safety foundation**.

It is **not**:

- legal certification  
- emergency response  
- custody adjudication  
- comprehensive abuse-prevention assurance  
- COPPA / FERPA / GDPR-K compliance  
- law-enforcement integration  
- production readiness for real minors  

Production use—especially involving minors—requires jurisdiction-specific legal review, trained human operations, incident-response procedures, abuse-reporting policy, escalation policy, provider validation, and substantially broader adversarial testing.
