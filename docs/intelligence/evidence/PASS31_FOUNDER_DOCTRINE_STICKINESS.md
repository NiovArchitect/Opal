## Internal shorthand (never public product copy)

**“Hooked and booked”** means:

> Social possibility so relevant and actionable that people return because Opal keeps helping them **live** more of their actual lives.

Loop:

**HOOKED BY POSSIBILITY → ALIGNED WITH PEOPLE → SET / COMMITTED / BOOKED (broad sense) → REAL EXPERIENCE → SHARE → NEW POSSIBILITY → GO AGAIN**

“Booked” ≠ only commercial reservation. Beach walk, hike, church lunch, family visit can be **set**.

## Relationship classes

| Class | Meaning | Does **not** grant |
|-------|---------|---------------------|
| **Follow** | I want eligible content from this person in discovery | phone, chat, calendar, Reality, booking, payment |
| **Connection / friend** | Real relationship context under permissions | unlimited authority |
| **Local discovery** | Stranger content may help me understand a place | friendship, private data, authority |

---

## Stickiness laws

- Stickiness from **life getting better**, not dark patterns.  
- Continuous discovery is a **real capability**, not “dumb scroll.”  
- Relevance appears quickly; exploration may continue fluidly.  
- Ranking target ≈ **lived-experience potential**, not consumption.  
- Free experiences as valid as monetizable.  
- Monetization after social behavior works.  
- Capability may be shared without raw PII.  
- Transportation / travel / reservation = consequences of **same Reality**.
