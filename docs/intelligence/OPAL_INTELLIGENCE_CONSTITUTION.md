---

## 1. Purpose

Opal is relationship and social-coordination intelligence. Humans talk. Opal derives one evolving Shared Reality and the next useful intervention.

This constitution exists so intelligence **compounds** across agents and PRs instead of being rewritten as conversational memory.

### Primary law

**INTELLIGENCE MUST COMPOUND.**

New capability may add. It must not silently delete, bypass, flatten, or replace existing intelligence without explicit evidence and a SUPERSEDED decision.

---

## 2. Evidence

Evidence is what actually happened:

- human messages  
- membership / participation  
- private availability windows (not disclosed raw)  
- provider free/busy outcomes  
- explicit accepts/declines  
- block / revoke  

Evidence is not inference. Store and cite evidence identifiers where domain already supports (`source_message_ids`, chronology, availability grants).

**Domain anchors:** messaging, `Chronology`, `Availability*`, invitations, TrustSafety.

---

## 3. Inference

Inference is what evidence may mean:

- Dinner intent  
- Thursday · 6:30 candidate  
- place still open  
- remote activity  

Inference is provisional until authority allows settlement. LLMs may propose inference; Elixir remains authoritative for product truth.

**Domain anchors:** `SharedRealityPresentation`, `SocialReality`, ProductSignals, Python proposal paths only.

---

## 4. Authority

Authority is what Opal may conclude or do:

- candidate only  
- private suggestion  
- shared possibility  
- settled dimension  
- authorized external write  

Never treat inference as settled plan without authority.

**Domain anchors:** `AlignmentAuthority`, participation models, `authorizes_set: false` on projections, SF15 session product auth.

---

## 5. Privacy

Private assistance stays private until explicit share.

- Calendar detail never crosses participant boundary as raw events.  
- Private place selection does not message peers.  
- Extend selection is private-first.  
- Only explicit Share (or clear send) crosses the relationship boundary.  

**Domain anchors:** AvailabilityComposition, private Extend, private chronology visibility, TrustSafety.

---

## 6. Social Reality

One evolving Shared Reality per social lineage:

**WHO · WHAT · WHEN · WHERE · PARTICIPATION · AUTHORITY · CONTEXT · EXECUTION · MEMORY · LINEAGE**

Dimensions may resolve in any order. No hard-coded single workflow.

Transport (SF15) carries messages. V2 Social Reality interprets them. Do not fork a second messaging system for intelligence.

**Domain anchors:** `SocialFlow.SocialReality`, Shared Reality presentation, ProductSignals.

---

## 7. Memory

Consequential transitions and relationship-relevant outcomes may be remembered.

- Prefer durable chronology for consequential shifts.  
- Do not dump full private reasoning as chat history.  
- Adaptive memory (merged main capabilities) is additive; it must not invent settled plans.  

---

## 8. Recomposition

After any mutation, recompute the whole picture:

- preserve still-true dimensions  
- reopen only what evidence invalidates  
- same lineage, no duplicate plan objects for maturation  

Example: “Actually Friday is better” → WHEN changes; WHAT/WHERE preserved unless invalidating evidence.

**Domain anchors:** `apply_dimension_update` / partial preservation tests, group recompose.

---

## 9. Next Gap

Exactly one meaningful `next_gap` for the current reality.

CTA is a consequence of reality, not:

- lastOpenedPanel  
- previousFlowMode  
- staleDraftType  

Order-agnostic: time→place and place→time are both valid.

**Domain anchors:** `SocialReality.next_meaningful_gap/3`, client `socialReality.ts`, gap-aware grammar.

---

## 10. Human Authorship

Humans remain authors of social commitment.

- Draft-first share is valid.  
- Opal may compose private options.  
- Explicit send/share is intentional.  
- Selection ≠ sending.  

---

## 11. Shared vs Private

| Class | Who sees | Examples |
|-------|----------|----------|
| Shared consequence | Conversation members | “Thursday · 6:30 lines up” |
| Private assistance | Viewer only | Only-you place sheet, private Extend |
| System/private calendar | Never peers as raw detail | Free/busy composition |

---

## 12. Calendar

Calendar feeds **WHEN**.

- Access ≠ disclosure.  
- Composition yields minimal human consequence.  
- No calendar-grid product identity.  
- Open-ended social time remains valid.  

**Domain anchors:** `AvailabilityComposition`, freebusy contracts, calendar privacy addendum evidence.

---

## 13. Place

Place is first-class.

- After WHEN sufficiently resolved and WHERE matters → place gap.  
- Private place UI; share place payload never time-only.  
- Remote activities: WHERE N/A — no fake venue.  
- Fixed events: no invent find-a-time/place search.  

**Domain anchors:** `PlaceGap`, place sheet, `buildPlaceShareDraft` / asserts.

---

## 14. Time

- Natural human time (Thu · 6:30 PM).  
- Open-ended windows valid.  
- One fact once in local presentation.  
- AM/PM where needed.  

**Domain anchors:** `composeHumanReality`, availability windows, SocialReality time gap.

---

## 15. Group

- Add person without restarting plan.  
- Recompute only affected dimensions.  
- Required vs optional participation.  
- No dyad proxy for true multi-party.  

**Domain anchors:** GroupComposition, group membership, scenario matrix.

---

## 16. Curate

- Gap-aware: if place open and time known, solve place/experience — not re-schedule.  
- Becomes the **same** Shared Reality object.  
- Private until human leads.  

---

## 17. Extend

- From active Shared Reality.  
- Private-first, reversible.  
- No auto-message; explicit share separate.  

---

## 18. Temporal

One object matures: Thursday → Tomorrow → Tonight → leave-by → happening → recall.

Leave-by only with real travel truth. No fabricated ETA.

---

## 19. Providers

Provider truth is evidence. Failures recompute; never invent bookings/settlements.

Python proposes; Elixir validates.

---

## 20. Execution

Execution (nav, booking handoff, reminders) is downstream of settled reality + authority. Handoffs must not claim completion Opal did not perform.

---

## 21. Failure Semantics

- Quiet when thin.  
- Prefer smallest useful output.  
- Distinguish PRODUCT_FAIL vs FIXTURE_FAIL vs ENVIRONMENT_FAIL in proof harness.  

---

## 22. Learning

Scoped, privacy-safe, non-hallucinatory. Must not rewrite Shared Reality without evidence.

---

## 23. Presentation

- Human-primary Chat.  
- Sparse consequential Opal filaments.  
- One fact once per local object.  
- Home / Chat / SR / Plans may differ in density; not in core truth.  
- Figma V2 presentation source remains locked for layout.  

**Domain anchors:** grammar, composeHumanReality, Opal filaments, presenceLines.
