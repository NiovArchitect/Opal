# ADR-INT-001 — Open-ended social time

**Status:** ACCEPTED  
**Date:** 2026-08-13 (codified; behavior pre-existing)

## Decision

Social availability may be open-ended (e.g. “after 6”, “Thursday evening”) without a hard end time. Opal must not force closed calendar intervals for human coordination.

## Consequences

- Availability windows may omit end.  
- Presentation may compress open-ended time humanly.  
- Calendar composition must not require fabricated ends.

## Anchors

Availability windows, AvailabilityComposition, SocialReality time gaps.
