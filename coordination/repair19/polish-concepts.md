"How well do you know [Name]?" - a gamified relationship-knowledge layer
whose engine is MEMORY VALIDATION. Designed concept for the next build
phase; recorded so it cannot evaporate. Build sequencing = post-walk phase.

Mechanics as perfected with the founder:
1. Questions draw ONLY on preference/memory the subject has made visible
   to the asker's scope - the scoped context builder is the quizmaster;
   a question can never leak out-of-scope data (scope law is the
   mechanic's foundation).
2. Modes: KNOW THEM (answer about a close person - proximity/relationship
   ranked), ABOUT YOU (validate or correct what is held about you -
   first-party confirmation, the data-quality gold), CIRCLE TRIVIA
   (mutual, inside a circle).
3. Guesses are labeled guesses - a wrong guess NEVER overwrites truth;
   only the subject validates truth, which updates the capsule.
   Validation requests anonymous by default; the guesser may reveal
   themselves, turning it into a shared moment.
4. Presentation: the spin - a wheel across your people's faces/colors
   with the casino-light language at play-surface intensity (earned
   excitement; QUIET still caps cadence). Points for knowing and for
   validating; reward terms hook into the wallet value loop -
   founder-gated commercial model.
5. Placement: lives on Home/social as a living card (its stage) + a
   doorway in each person's orbit (its per-person home).
6. Sensitivity gates: no quizzing on deceased/estranged ties; minors'
   questions stay inside family scope under the age-band policy
   (age-band policy calls are founder-gated - see the wave record).
