## Mission

Make the first five seconds unforgettable and the member product sustainable. Combine:

1. **Distinctive frontend design** — not cream/serif/terracotta defaults; not acid cyberpunk defaults; not “every AI app” glassmorphism
2. **Opal Technicolor system** — Full walkthrough / Controlled product

## Product doctrine

- Opal is multi-person social intelligence + real-world movement — **not** messenger, calendar, chatbot, or feed
- Dopamine from **social progress and alignment**, not streaks/points/rankings
- Human bubbles calm; Opal moments spectral and unmistakable
- Public copy age-12 readable; architecture language never user-facing
- North star: **speed to authentic alignment**

## Anti-templated-AI-UI checklist (fail the pass if any dominate)

- Cream background + generic serif + terracotta accent as default “warm”
- Near-black + single acid green as default “tech”
- Broadsheet hairlines with no product reason
- Giant centered orb with wordmark as meek caption
- Timid ghost Join button
- Unlabeled “success” chips that look booked
- Decorative gradients that don’t encode meaning (spark / proposal / handled)
