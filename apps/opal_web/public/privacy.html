Privacy

Last updated: August 2026 · Product summary (not a legal claim of identity or certification)

What Opal is

Opal helps people align on real plans with people they know. We do not claim to be a bank, government identity service, or background-check product.

Phone number and one-time codes

When you ask for a sign-in code, you confirm we may text that number a one-time security code. That consent is for authentication only — not marketing. Message and data rates may apply. We store a protected form of your number for account security; we do not put your phone number in invite links or public URLs.

Invitations and relationships

Invites are for people you choose to connect with. Opening an invite does not publish your private answers. Relationship and conversation membership are controlled on the server.

Private participation

Some alignment responses stay private to you. Peers may see shared-safe progress labels (for example “Still open”), not your raw private answer or reason.

Messages

Conversation messages are for participants in that conversation. We do not use your message bodies as marketing copy.

Contact

Questions about this summary: use the contact path published by NIOV Labs for Opal product support. This page describes current product behavior and may be refined as the product ships.
