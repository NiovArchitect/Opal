Your agents move the relationships forward. Atrium helps keep the work connected.

An agent’s clients feel looked after when nothing they said gets lost. The brokerage sees that standard being met in the work itself: conversations kept, next steps standing, files in order.

Compliance, in the open

The broker can see into the brokerage’s own graphs, because the clients are the brokerage’s clients.

The real estate agents who serve those clients work for the broker. So the broker’s view into the graphed conversations of the brokerage’s own work is compliance oversight: it is how the brokerage stands behind every file and answers for it. That is not surveillance, and it carries none of that energy. It is responsibility, made visible, and it is part of what keeps Atrium operating at its best. Inside that view, things come together the way the relationships themselves do: agents look back into their own previous conversations, the broker sees what compliance needs to see, and Atrium’s AI Assist works on every side to help the people in a relationship collaborate. The fences stay exact where they belong: nothing crosses to another brokerage, and nothing reaches a client outside the relationship it was said in.

Stated plainly

The brokerage being built on Atrium is proposed, not operating.

Atrium Realty is a name still to be cleared, not a brokerage anyone can join today. The shape of it is one broker per state: one broker answerable for the state’s whole book, and this page speaks to that broker. What stands today is the product: the anchored files, the queue, the gate. When the proposed brokerage opens, this page will say so, in those words.
