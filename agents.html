After the call

The call ends. What it decided stays standing.

Every graphed call leaves its recap in its own conversation: what mattered, what changed, what is next. And you can look back into your own previous conversations whenever the work needs them, because the record stands where the relationship put it.

The fence

What sits outside the relationship stays outside it.

Another client’s words are not yours to see. A client’s private browsing and private choices are not a feed. When you ask for what a relationship does not hold, the door refuses, by name. Access follows roles and permissions, yours included. Inside the brokerage, the broker who stands behind your work can see into the brokerage’s own graphed record to ensure compliance, because the clients are the brokerage’s clients. That oversight is part of the relationship, and it is stated in the open. It reaches nothing outside the brokerage, and nothing outside the relationships it governs. The same fence is what lets your clients trust you with what they say.
